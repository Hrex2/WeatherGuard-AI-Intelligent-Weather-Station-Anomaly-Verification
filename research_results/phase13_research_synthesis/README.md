# Phase 13 research synthesis package

This folder consolidates evidence from WeatherGuard AI Phases 01–12. The authoritative Phase 12 analysis is `research_results/phase12_evidence_contribution/20261119T144632Z/`; the eight PNGs under `figures/` are copies of that run's visualizations. Original experiment outputs were preserved.

## Files

- `PHASE13_FINAL_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md` — research questions, dataset/method synthesis, results, interpretation, references, and embedded figures.
- `PHASE13_PHASE_TRACEABILITY.csv` — primary artifact paths and outcomes for Phases 01–12.
- `PHASE13_SCIENTIFIC_CLAIMS.csv` — claim, evidence source, strength, status, and limitations.
- `PHASE13_EXPERIMENTAL_RESULTS.csv` — verified numerical findings and interpretation limits.
- `PHASE13_LIMITATIONS_AND_FUTURE_WORK.md` — limitations and ranked next steps.
- `PHASE13_COMPLETION_ASSESSMENT.md` — readiness, reconciliations, and recommended next report task.
- `FIGURE_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv` — captions and exact source artifact for each copied figure.

## Figure provenance

All figures are unchanged copies from the authoritative Phase 12 run and depict descriptive evidence coverage, component overlap/dependency, sensitivity profiles, E3 timestamp offsets, or missingness. They do not show independent event truth or model accuracy.

## Unresolved research gaps

Independent adjudicated fault/event labels, original provider report IDs and raw observation lineage, verified station/instrument independence, event-level E3 matching, and the 42/100,980 Isolation Forest prediction parity mismatches remain unresolved. Consequently no accuracy, recall, false-alarm, or validated E3-improvement claim is supported. **ANOMALY ≠ FAULT.**
